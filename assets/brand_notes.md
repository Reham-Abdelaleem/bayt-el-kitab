# Brand Guidelines: Bayt El Kitab (بيت الكتاب)

**Prepared for:** TaqadumEd Front-End Consultant  
**Client:** Tariq El-Sayed (Owner, Bayt El Kitab)  
**Date:** April 2026  
**Location:** Alexandria, Egypt  

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Overview
Bayt El Kitab is not just a bookstore; it is a cultural sanctuary nestled in the heart of Alexandria. For decades, it has served as a gathering place for lovers of literature, history, and poetry. As we transition to our first digital MVP ahead of the Alexandria Summer Reading Festival, our online presence must reflect the warmth, intellect, and heritage of our physical shop. 

The digital storefront should feel exactly like walking into our physical store: greeted by the smell of old paper, the warmth of mahogany shelves, and a friendly, knowledgeable bookseller.

## 2. Color Palette
Our colors are inspired by the Mediterranean sea of Alexandria, the aged paper of vintage books, and the warm wood of our library shelves.

### Primary Colors
- **Mediterranean Navy:** `#1A365D`
  *Usage:* Main text, headers, primary buttons, and navigation. Represents depth, knowledge, and the Alexandrian sea.
- **Parchment White:** `#F9F6F0`
  *Usage:* Main background color. Much softer on the eyes than pure white, mimicking the pages of a well-loved novel and ensuring comfortable reading.

### Secondary & Accent Colors
- **Terracotta Red:** `#C05640`
  *Usage:* Call-to-action buttons (e.g., "Add to Order", "Search"), highlights, and price tags (EGP). Evokes the color of traditional Egyptian brickwork and adds a warm, inviting pop of color.
- **Olive Wood:** `#8A785E`
  *Usage:* Secondary text, borders, dividers, and footer backgrounds. Adds a grounded, earthy, and historical feel.

## 3. Typography
Since we cater to a community that values literature, our typography must be elegant, highly legible, and capable of supporting both Latin and Arabic scripts effortlessly.

- **Headings (Serif): `Lora` or `Playfair Display`**
  *Fallback: `Georgia`, `serif`*
  *Why:* Brings a classic, literary, and editorial feel to book titles, page headers, and the main logo text.
  
- **Body Text (Sans-Serif): `Cairo` or `Lato`**
  *Fallback: `Arial`, `sans-serif`*
  *Why:* `Cairo` is a beautiful modern font that supports both Arabic and English seamlessly. It is highly legible for book descriptions, interface elements, order forms, and navigation menus.

*Note: If you incorporate any localized Arabic text accents, we highly recommend using the `Amiri` font alongside `Cairo` for a classical, poetic touch.*

## 4. Tone of Voice
- **Welcoming & Personal:** We speak to our customers like they are guests in our home. Use phrases like "Welcome to our shelves," or "Let us help you find your next great read."
- **Knowledgeable but Accessible:** Avoid overly academic jargon. We love books, and we want to share that love without being pretentious.
- **Culturally Authentic:** Rooted in Egyptian heritage. Subtle nods to our location in Alexandria are highly encouraged.
- **Clear & Polite:** For the Order Form, keep instructions highly functional but warm. Example: "Please provide your delivery details below, and our staff will confirm your order and total EGP amount shortly."

## 5. UI/UX Directives for the MVP
- **Clean and Uncluttered:** We do not want flashing banners or cluttered layouts. Let the book titles and authors be the stars of the page.
- **Accessibility:** Ensure high contrast between text (Mediterranean Navy) and background (Parchment White). 
- **Buttons & Forms:** Input fields and buttons should have slightly rounded corners (`border-radius: 4px;` or `6px;`) to feel approachable rather than harsh and corporate.
