## Page 1: Home (`index.html`)

**Hero / Main Heading:**
Welcome to Bayt El Kitab

**Subheading:**
Alexandria's Haven for Literature, History, and Poetry

**Welcome Message:**
Welcome to Bayt El Kitab, your local independent bookshop nestled in the heart of Alexandria. For decades, our physical shelves have been a sanctuary for readers, writers, and thinkers. Now, as we prepare for the 2026 Alexandria Summer Reading Festival, we are thrilled to open our digital doors to book lovers across Egypt.

**Our Story (Section Heading):**
Our Story

**History Paragraph:**
Founded by the El-Sayed family, Bayt El Kitab began as a humble corner shop filled with rare finds, regional poetry, and beloved classics. Over the years, we have grown into a cultural landmark for Alexandria's literary community. Despite the changing times, we remain dedicated to our original mission: curating a thoughtful selection of books that inspire the mind and warm the soul. Whether you are looking for ancient Egyptian history, contemporary Arabic literature, or translated global classics, you will find a curated home here.
